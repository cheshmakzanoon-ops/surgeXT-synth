# Film Scoring Hans

A bank of 500 Surge XT patches for hybrid orchestral film scoring, in
`resources/data/patches_3rdparty/Film Scoring Hans`. They appear in the patch browser under
the third-party patches, in 20 categories:

| Category         | Presets | Category          | Presets |
|------------------|--------:|-------------------|--------:|
| Braams           |      25 | Percussion        |      30 |
| Horns & Brass    |      25 | Hits & Impacts    |      25 |
| String Ostinatos |      30 | Pianos & Keys     |      20 |
| String Swells    |      25 | Plucks & Mallets  |      20 |
| Drones           |      25 | Ethnic Winds      |      25 |
| Synth Basses     |      30 | Guitars           |      20 |
| Pulses & Ticking |      25 | 80s Synths        |      25 |
| Shepard & Risers |      25 | Atmospheres       |      30 |
| Organs           |      25 | Tension FX        |      25 |
| Choirs & Voices  |      25 | Leads & Themes    |      20 |

Each category has four to six base sounds, and each base comes in five variants (wider, darker,
driven, slower and so on). The bases are modelled on instruments and production techniques
that are documented in Hans Zimmer's scores: the low brass of *Inception*, the Temple Church
organ of *Interstellar*, the ticking watch and Shepard tones of *Dunkirk*, the duduk and voice
of *Gladiator*, the throat singing and bagpipes of *Dune*, the cimbalom and prepared piano of
*Sherlock Holmes*, the pedal steel guitars of *Man of Steel*, the all-synthesizer scores of the
late 1980s, and so on. They are original synthesizer patches written in that spirit, not
recreations of any recording. Every patch's comment says what it is modelled on, and
[PRESETS.md](PRESETS.md) lists them all.

Playing them:

- **Macros**: every patch has *Brightness* (filter cutoff), *Attack* (amp envelope attack) and
  *Space* (reverb send).
- **Mod wheel**: on sustained orchestral sounds (strings, brass, choirs, winds) it works as
  dynamics, opening the filter and lifting the level.
- **Hold to play rhythms**: most of the string ostinatos, the pulse ostinato bass and all the
  pulses retrigger their envelopes from a tempo-synced step sequencer, so a held chord plays in
  time with the host. Spiccato Engine and Two Note Marcato are short articulations you play
  yourself.
- **Shepard tones** use one falling sawtooth LFO to move three octave-spaced oscillators while
  the bottom one fades in and the top one fades out, so the pitch seems to rise (or fall)
  forever.

The patches are level-matched. A sustained single note aims for -16 dBFS RMS (-18 for drones
and atmospheres) and a short sound for -10 dBFS over its loudest 50 ms, but no single note may
peak above -3 dBFS and no four-note chord above -3.5 dBFS, so patches made for chords often
sit a few dB lower.

## Regenerating

The patches are generated by `generate.py` through the Surge Python bindings, so every patch
is streamed by the engine itself at the current patch revision, with its wavetables embedded.

```bash
cmake -S . -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DSURGE_BUILD_PYTHON_BINDINGS=ON
cmake --build build --target surgepy
PYTHONPATH=build/src/surge-python python3 scripts/film-scoring-hans/generate.py
```

`--only <text>` rebuilds the presets whose `folder/name` contains the text, and `--jobs`
sets the number of worker processes.

- `fsh_engine.py` turns a patch spec (oscillators, filters, envelopes, LFOs, effects and
  modulation in musical units) into a Surge patch, renders it, and writes the .fxp metadata.
- `fsh_recipes.py` is the sound library: the base sounds, the variants and the catalogue.
- `generate.py` builds each patch, reloads it from disk, renders it to check that it plays and
  to level-match it, saves it, then reloads and renders it once more to check what's on disk.
