# Film Scoring Hans presets

500 Surge XT patches for hybrid orchestral film scoring, generated by `generate.py`. They are original patches modelled on instruments and techniques documented in Hans Zimmer's scores; the notes say which.

All patches have three macros: **Brightness** (filter cutoff), **Attack** (amp envelope attack) and **Space** (reverb send). On sustained orchestral sounds the mod wheel works as dynamics. The tempo-synced patches (most of String Ostinatos, Pulse Ostinato Bass and all of Pulses & Ticking) play their rhythm for as long as a note is held.
